About

I'm a guy who likes programming, weight-lifting, fantasy, crypto, and other nerdy stuff.

Quick work history:

  • I obtained a PhD in computer science from the Université Catholique de Louvain in 2019.
  • Until 2021, I worked for Oracle Labs on TruffleRuby, the fastest Ruby implementation then & now.
  • In 2021 & 2022, I taught a compiler class.
  • Until early 2023, I worked for Optimism, where I was part of the team that built the OP Stack, most of the specs if which I wrote. It now secures upwards of 17 billions in value, as well as hundreds of millions in transacted volume every single day.
  • In 2024-2025, I ran my own startup. We built a gaming chain called HappyChain, which I had to shut it down for lack of investment, but we built a state of the art wallet and account abstraction stack over the span of one year.
