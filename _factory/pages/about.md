I'm [a guy][twitter] who likes programming, weight-lifting, fantasy, crypto, and other nerdy stuff.

Quick work history:

- I obtained a [PhD in computer science](/publications) from the Université Catholique
  de Louvain in 2019.
- Until 2021, I worked for Oracle Labs on [TruffleRuby][tr], the fastest Ruby implementation then & now.
- In 2021 & 2022, I taught a [compiler class].
- Until early 2023, I worked for Optimism, where I was part of the team that built the [OP Stack], most of the specs
  if which I wrote. It now secures [upwards of 17 billions][l2beat] in value, as well as [hundreds of millions][defillama]
  in transacted volume every single day.
- In 2024-2025, I ran my own startup. We built a gaming chain called [HappyChain], which I had to shut it down for lack
  of investment, but we built a state of the art [wallet] and [account abstraction stack][aa] over the span of one year.
